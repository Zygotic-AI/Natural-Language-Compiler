## Performance terms

- Performance = e/e = effectiveness and efficiency.
- AEE (alpha effectiveness and efficiency, written αee) = the acceleration of e/e. It means the rate of improvement is itself increasing.
- Delta-squared EE (Δ²EE) = the rate of change of e/e's rate of change. This is how AEE is measured.
